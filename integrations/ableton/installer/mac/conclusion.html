StageCue is ready in Ableton

  1. Open Live (if it was open, it may take a moment to show the new device).
  2. In Live's browser, open User Library › Presets › Audio Effects › Max Audio Effect › StageCue.
  3. Drag StageCue onto the master track. Audio passes through untouched.
  4. The light on the device turns green when it finds StageCue on the network.

To have it in every new song: with the device on the master track, open Live's Settings, File & Folder, and click Save Current Set as Default.
